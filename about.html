About

Built for learners who cannot — or will not — pay for every course

Hunar (from the Urdu/Persian for “skill”) is a free learning platform that structures career-oriented curricula around legal, publicly available resources: official documentation, free tools, open educational material, and educator-published videos.

What we offer

  • Web development — HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React foundations, GitHub Pages
  • UI/UX design — research, wireframes, Figma, usability testing
  • Graphics design — hierarchy, typography, Photopea, Inkscape
  • Digital marketing and SEO — strategy, content, Search Console
  • Content writing and app store optimization
  • Unity and Blender fundamentals
  • Video editing with DaVinci Resolve Free

Resource policy

We link only to resources that are free to access without circumventing paywalls. We do not host or distribute pirated or leaked paid courses.

Privacy and progress

Progress is stored in your browser (localStorage). No account is required. Clearing site data resets progress.

Languages

Interface and lesson content support professional English and natural Roman Urdu for regional accessibility alongside international use.
